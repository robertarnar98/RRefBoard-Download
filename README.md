# RRefBoard

A moodboard for your reference pictures on the Mac. Drop in photos (RAW too), videos, PDFs, audio, YouTube and web links, notes and sketches, arrange them on an endless board, and save the whole board as one `.rref` file you can send to anyone with RRefBoard.

## Download

**[⬇︎ Download the newest version](https://github.com/robertarnar98/RRefBoard-Download/releases/latest)**: click **RRefBoard.dmg** under *Assets*.

## Install (one time)

1. Double-click **RRefBoard.dmg**.
2. Drag **RRefBoard** onto the **Applications** folder.
3. Open it from Applications. RRefBoard is signed and approved by Apple, so it opens with no security warning.

## Updates

You don't need to come back here. When there's a new version, a card pops up in the middle of RRefBoard. Click **Update Now** and it updates itself in a few seconds and reopens, with your open tabs. You can also choose **RRefBoard ▸ Check for Updates…** any time.

## Privacy

RRefBoard collects nothing: no accounts, no analytics, no tracking, no ads. Your pictures and boards stay on your Mac.

It looks for updates on this page (that check sends nothing about you, your Mac or your boards), and otherwise only goes online for link cards and YouTube cards: once per link, to fetch that page's preview picture and title, and YouTube's preview and player for video cards. It never contacts devices on your own network (router, printer, other computers), even if a board file points there. The full privacy policy is in the app under **Help**. GitHub, which hosts this page and the downloads, sees your download like any web visit ([GitHub's privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement)).

## Board files from other people

Open `.rref` files only from people you trust, like any document. RRefBoard checks everything inside a board when it opens it: every picture must match its fingerprint, links must be ordinary web pages, and documents (PDFs, sheets) open as copies in their own app, never as programs.
